Imputing and Cleaning Job Roles¶

  • I elected to impute job title in for missing roles since my analysis indicated that the two have been used interchangably over time.

  • I've replaced common acronymns related to leadership to ensure consitant identification during training

In [12]:
# consolidate role-title
aff['role_imputed'] = aff['role'].fillna(aff['title'])
